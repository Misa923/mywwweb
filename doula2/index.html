About Me

Warm, inclusive, and practical support for every family.

Hi, my name is Robin, owner of Robin Song.

I’m a Certified Birth Doula, wife, and grandmother of four little boys.

Every Birth sings its own Song.

Helping a woman rise into confidence in childbirth is what I love about being a doula. I want all mothers to feel informed, supported and safe — and strong in asking for what they need for their pregnancy and childbirth experiences. Birth is surprising, deeply intense and transformational, and I am here to stand beside you and your family all the way, ensuring you have the support, love and information that will allow you to flourish.
