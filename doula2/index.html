About Me

A calm presence through life’s biggest transitions.

I’m a Certified Birth Doula, wife, and grandmother of four little boys.

I fell in love with childbirth giving birth at home, and later I apprenticed with homebirth midwives and became a doula in 2015. I provide services throughout the Ozarks, including Northwest Arkansas and southernmost Missouri.

Training & Tools

  • Birth Doula, trained through DONA and Ujima Maternity Services (Certified)
  • Certified Breastfeeding Counselor
  • Hypnodoula, trained through Hypnobabies®
  • CPR (Infant & Adult)
  • HIPAA

Services

Prenatal

I offer a full spectrum of services throughout pregnancy, based on your family’s needs. This includes planning and preparation, education, emotional support and being on call for your labor.

Birth

I am a non-clinical companion for you and your partner — building confidence, advocating for you to experience the most comfortable and satisfying birth experience possible, and helping you navigate any challenges.

Postpartum

I am here to help you remember and reflect on your birth experience, to keep track of the timeline, and provide gentle guidance for a robust, healthy breastfeeding experience with your baby. I can provide resources for your postpartum needs and concerns.
