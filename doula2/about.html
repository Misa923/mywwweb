About Me

A balanced, client‑centered approach to doula support.

I believe every body and every birth is unique. My role is to help you clarify your preferences, prepare for options, and create a calm, confident environment for labor, birth, and postpartum.

I support families in hospitals, birth centers, and at home—medicated, unmedicated, VBAC, and more.

Certifications

  • DONA Birth Doula
  • Certified Lactation Counselor (CLC)
  • Placenta Encapsulation Specialist
  • Prenatal & Postpartum Yoga
