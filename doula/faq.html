Frequently Asked Questions

What type of doula are you?

Balanced and client‑centered. I adapt tools and techniques to your needs and preferences.

Do you take the place of my partner?

No. I support the birthing person and their partner, helping everyone feel confident and connected.

How many birth clients do you take per month?

A limited caseload to ensure personalized support.

Do you support all types of birth?

Yes — hospital, birth center, home, medicated, unmedicated, VBAC, and more. Your birth, your choices.
